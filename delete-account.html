Deleting your data

App: Balin (com.athul.balin)

Balin has no account and no sign-in, so there's nothing on the developer's end to delete — every copy of your data already lives somewhere you control.

On your phone

Open Balin → Settings → Delete & advanced to wipe specific history, or just uninstall the app — that removes everything Balin stored on the phone.

A backup you turned on

If you ever switched on a backup destination in Settings → Your data:

  • Your own server: delete the backup file on that server — it's yours.
  • Your own Google Drive: go to your Google Account → Manage third-party access, find Balin, and remove it. That deletes the hidden app-data folder Balin wrote to.
