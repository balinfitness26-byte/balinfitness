Privacy Policy for Balin

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Balin ("the app") is a personal fitness, nutrition, sleep and habit-tracking app. This policy explains what data the app handles and where it goes. The short version: your logs and personal details stay on your own phone, or on a server you choose and control yourself — not on a server the developer runs or has access to.

1. Data stored only on your device

All of the following stays in local storage on your phone and is never sent anywhere unless you explicitly choose to back it up (see Section 2):

  • Your name, age, sex, weight, height, country/region, and fitness goals
  • Food, water and workout logs
  • Sleep and chronotype data, alarm and reminder settings
  • App passcode (stored as a hash on-device; never leaves the phone)
  • Any AI server address/access key, and any backup server address/access key you enter in Settings

Uninstalling the app deletes all of this. There is no account to create and no login — the developer has no copy of this data and no way to access it.

2. Optional backup — your choice of destination

Settings → Your data lets you choose where backups go:

  • Local only (default): nothing is uploaded anywhere. You can export a CSV copy yourself at any time.
  • Your own server: if you enter a server address (for example a NAS, a home server, or a small VPS you run), the app sends your backup directly to that address, encrypted in transit via HTTPS. That server is yours — the developer never has access to it or to what's stored there.
  • Google Drive: planned for a future release; not yet functional.

3. The AI features

Balin's AI coaching, chat, diet suggestions and voice features work by sending your question or relevant log data to an AI server address you provide in Settings → AI engine (for example, your own locally-hosted model, or another compatible service of your choosing). The developer does not operate or have access to this server, and no AI processing happens unless you've configured one yourself. Responsibility for how that chosen server handles the data it receives rests with whoever operates it — read that service's own privacy terms if it's a third party.

4. Anonymous, shared food and workout database

When you log a food or workout that isn't already in the app's built-in list, the app may contribute that item's name and nutrition/exercise details to a shared, anonymous database (hosted on Google Firebase) so other users searching for the same item don't have to enter it from scratch. This only ever includes the food/workout name and its nutritional or exercise values — never your personal logs, your name, or any data that identifies you. It cannot be traced back to you.

5. Crash reporting

The app uses Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase App Check to detect crashes and confirm requests come from a genuine copy of the app. This can include basic device information (model, OS version) and a stack trace at the moment of a crash. It does not include your personal logs or account data (there is no account). You can see Google's own handling of this data at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

6. What the developer never does

  • Never sells or shares your personal data with advertisers or third parties
  • Never has access to your logs, your AI server, or your backup server
  • Does not show ads
  • Does not require an account or login of any kind

7. Children

Balin is intended for users aged 14 and up, and the app enforces this at setup. It is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

8. Your choices

  • Delete all local data: uninstall the app, or use Settings → Delete & advanced to wipe specific history.
  • Delete a self-hosted backup: delete the file/record on your own server — the developer has no copy to delete.
  • Stop AI or backup features at any time by clearing the address fields in Settings.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change and, for any material change, notice will be given inside the app.
