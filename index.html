Balin Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026 · App: Balin (com.athul.balin)

Balin is a personal fitness, nutrition, sleep and habit-tracking app with an optional AI coach. This page explains what the app stores and what, if anything, leaves your phone. In short: there is no Balin account and no sign-in; your logs live on your phone; they leave it only if you turn on a backup destination you pick yourself, or if you connect an AI feature to a server you choose. Nothing is sold, and there are no ads and no analytics.

1. What the app stores on your phone

  • Profile details you enter: name, birth year, sex, height, weight, body measurements, goals and units.
  • Your logs: workouts, meals and water, daily check-ins (sleep, soreness, energy), protocols and training programs.
  • Settings, reminders, and your app passcode (stored only as a salted, one-way hash, never in readable form).
  • Any AI server address/access key or API key, and any backup server address/key you enter in Settings — these stay on this phone and are never included in any backup.

Uninstalling the app deletes all of this. There is no account to create and no login of any kind — the developer has no copy of this data and no way to access it. (Android's own device backup, if you have it switched on in Android's own settings, may copy app data to your Google account; that is controlled by Android and your Google account, not by Balin.)

2. Optional backup — a destination you choose

Settings → Your data lets you choose where backups go. Whichever you pick, the developer has no access to it:

  • Local only (default): nothing is uploaded anywhere. You can export a CSV copy yourself at any time, and Balin reminds you to keep that copy current.
  • Your own server: if you enter a server address (for example a NAS, a home server, or a small VPS you run), the app sends your backup directly to that address over HTTPS. That server is yours.
  • Your own Google Drive: you can sign in with Google and let Balin back up to a hidden, app-only folder in your own Drive (the "appdata" space) that only Balin can see — not the rest of your Drive. Only your Google account can read it.

3. The AI features (optional, off until you set one up)

Balin's AI coach, Chat with Balin, food and exercise lookups, meal ideas, the readiness check and the program builder only work once you connect an AI engine in Settings → AI engine. There are two options, both configured by you, not the developer:

  • A self-hosted server: your own AI gateway in front of a model you run yourself (for example Ollama), or any compatible server you point the app at. The address, access key and model name you enter stay on your phone.
  • A direct API key: a base address, API key and model name for any provider whose API speaks the OpenAI chat-completions format (OpenAI, Groq, Together, Mistral, OpenRouter, DeepSeek, and others). Requests go straight from your phone to that provider, with no server in between.

Either way, a request can include what you typed and the profile/log details needed to answer it (for example age, sex, weight, recent calories, recent training or sleep). The developer does not operate either kind of AI engine and never sees these requests unless you've pointed the app at a server you yourself run. Responsibility for how a chosen server or provider handles what it receives rests with whoever operates it — read that provider's own privacy terms if it's a third party. AI answers can be wrong: they are general information, not medical advice.

4. Health Connect (optional)

If you allow it, Balin reads your daily step count and sleep sessions from Health Connect. It never writes to Health Connect. Steps estimate your daily activity; sleep times feed your recovery check-in and bedtime reminders. This data is used only inside Balin, stays on your phone (it may be included if you turn on your own chosen backup destination), and is never used for advertising. You can withdraw access at any time in Health Connect or in Balin's Settings.

5. Calendar (optional)

If you turn on holiday-aware Smart Wake, Balin reads your calendar to check whether a day is a holiday or marked as leave. It only checks event dates/titles for that purpose — nothing beyond "is this day a holiday" is kept, and nothing leaves your phone. You can withdraw calendar access at any time in Android Settings or in Balin's Settings.

6. Phone usage access (optional)

If you switch on automatic sleep detection and grant "Usage access", Balin looks at when your phone was last used at night and first used in the morning to estimate your bedtime and wake time. Only those times are kept, on your phone. Which apps you used is not stored or sent anywhere.

7. Anonymous, shared food and exercise database

When you log a food or workout that isn't already in the app's built-in list, the app may contribute that item's name and nutrition or exercise values to a shared, anonymous database (hosted on Google Firebase) so other users searching for the same item don't have to enter it from scratch. This only ever includes the food/workout name and its nutritional or exercise values — never your personal logs, your name, or anything that identifies you. It can't be traced back to you, and nothing you log privately is ever included.

8. Crash reports and app integrity

The app uses Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase App Check. Crashlytics sends a crash report (the error, device model, Android version and a random installation ID — not linked to your name or any account) so problems can be fixed; it never includes your logs or profile. App Check confirms requests to the shared database in Section 6 come from a genuine copy of the app, without needing a sign-in. See Google's own handling of this data at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

9. What the developer never does

  • No advertising, no ad tracking, no advertising ID.
  • No analytics on what you do in the app.
  • Never sells or shares your data with advertisers or third parties.
  • Never has access to your logs, your AI server, or your backup destination.
  • No account or login of any kind to create, lose, or have breached.

11. Deleting your data

There is no Balin account, so there is nothing to delete on the developer's end — every copy of your data is somewhere you control (a step-by-step version of this section is also at delete-account.html):

  • On your phone: Settings → Delete & advanced lets you wipe specific history; uninstalling the app removes everything stored on the phone.
  • A backup you turned on: delete the file on your own server, or remove Balin's app-data folder from your Google Drive (Google Account → Manage third-party access) — the developer has no copy to delete.
  • Health Connect: remove Balin's access in Health Connect at any time.
  • Calendar: remove Balin's calendar access in Android Settings at any time.

12. Children

Balin is intended for users aged 14 and up, and the app enforces this at setup. It is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.
