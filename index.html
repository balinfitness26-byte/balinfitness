Balin Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026 · App: Balin (com.athul.balin)

Balin is a fitness and nutrition tracker with an AI coach. This page explains what the app stores, what leaves your phone, and why. In short: your logs live on your phone; they go to the cloud only if you turn on cloud backup; AI features send the details needed to answer you to Balin's own AI server (Kattu, run by the developer); nothing is sold and there are no ads.

1. What the app stores on your phone

  • Profile details you enter: name, birth year, sex, height, weight, body measurements, goals and units.
  • Your logs: workouts, meals and water, daily check-ins (sleep, soreness, energy), protocols and training programs.
  • Settings, reminders and your app passcode (stored only as a salted, one-way hash, never in readable form).

This data stays on your device. Android's own backup may copy it to your Google account if you have device backup switched on; that backup is controlled by Android and your Google account, not by us.

2. Health Connect

If you allow it, Balin reads your daily step count and sleep sessions from Health Connect. It never writes to Health Connect. Steps estimate your daily activity; sleep times feed your recovery check-in and bedtime reminders. When you ask the AI coach for a readiness check, your sleep hours may be included in that request (see section 4).

Data from Health Connect is used only to provide these features inside Balin. It is not used for advertising, not sold, and not shared with anyone except as described here to provide a feature you asked for. You can withdraw access at any time in Health Connect or in Balin's Settings.

3. Phone usage access (optional)

If you switch on automatic sleep detection and grant "Usage access", Balin looks at when your phone was last used at night and first used in the morning to estimate your bedtime and wake time. Only those times are kept, on your phone. Which apps you used is not stored or sent anywhere.

4. AI features (Kattu, the developer's own AI server)

Balin's AI coach, food and exercise lookups, meal ideas, readiness check and program builder send your request to Kattu, an AI server run by Balin's developer on their own computer (an open-source Llama model through Ollama). No Google, OpenAI or other AI company receives these requests. Depending on the feature, a request can include what you typed, the food or exercise name, and the profile and log details needed to answer (for example age, sex, weight, height, body-fat estimate, recent calories, recent training and sleep). Requests travel over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection, through Tailscale's network service, which carries the encrypted traffic but can't read it.

The server answers your request and does not save your question or its answer. It keeps a short technical log (time, a random ID for your app install, request size and how long it took) to share the server fairly and spot misuse. The random ID isn't linked to your name, email or account. AI answers can be wrong: they are general information, not medical advice. You can report a bad answer with the "Report" link under it.

5. Cloud backup (optional)

If you create a Balin account (email and password, handled by Firebase Authentication), your logs, custom foods and exercises, and settings are backed up to Google Cloud Firestore so you can restore them on another phone. Only you can read your backup; it is protected by your account and our database security rules. Your passcode is never included.

6. Shared food and exercise list

When you're logged in and add a custom food or exercise that isn't in Balin's list yet, its name and nutrition or intensity values are added to a shared list that other Balin users can search. Nothing that identifies you is attached to it.

7. Crash reports

If the app crashes, Firebase Crashlytics sends a crash report (the error, device model, Android version and an installation ID) so the problem can be fixed. It doesn't include your logs or profile.

8. What we don't do

  • No advertising, no ad tracking, no advertising ID.
  • No selling or renting of your data.
  • No analytics on what you do in the app.

10. Deleting your data

  • On your phone: Settings in Balin lets you delete workout and nutrition history; uninstalling the app removes everything stored on the phone.
  • Cloud account and backup: Balin → Settings → Cloud backup → Delete cloud account. This deletes your backup and your account immediately. If you no longer have the app, see how to request deletion.
  • Health Connect: remove Balin's access in Health Connect at any time.

11. Age

Balin is not meant for children under 14 and the app asks for your birth year to enforce this.
